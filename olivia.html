The Discography

Olivia Rodrigo didn't just enter the music industry; she redirected it. Her debut single "drivers license" broke nearly every streaming record in existence, proving that her vulnerable, diary-entry style of songwriting resonated with millions. Following her debut, she proved she wasn't a one-hit wonder with the release of her second album, GUTS, which leaned into a 90s-inspired pop-punk sound.

  1. SOUR (2021) - A heartbreak masterpiece.
    • drivers license: The song that started the phenomenon.
    • good 4 u: A pop-punk anthem for the ages.
    • traitor: A hauntingly beautiful ballad.
  2. GUTS (2023) - Growing up and getting loud.
    • vampire: A theatrical masterpiece about fame and betrayal.
    • bad idea right?: A fun, messy look at returning to an ex.
    • get him back!: A witty, drum-heavy chant.
  3. Top 3 Personal Picks - My personal rotation:
    • obsessed: The ultimate "retrospective jealousy" anthem.
    • one step forward, three steps back: Emotional piano perfection.
    • vampire: A vocal powerhouse performance.

Featured Music Video

The music video for "vampire" was directed by Petra Collins and features a stunning, dream-like aesthetic that starts in a field and ends on a chaotic stage, perfectly mirroring the song's crescendo.

Relationship Update ☕

Olivia’s lyrics have always been famously transparent. Fans spent years decoding the SOUR love triangle, but the GUTS era seems to show a more mature Olivia. Currently, she has been spotted frequently with actor Louis Partridge.

They have been seen together in London and New York, and fans are thrilled to see Olivia looking happy and thriving. This is a refreshing change of pace from the "traitor" era heartbreaks!
